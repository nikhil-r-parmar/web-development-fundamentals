   NPCCSM, a constituent college of Kadi Sarva Vishwavidyalaya (KSV), Gandhinagar, is committed to delivering high-quality education in computer applications and information technology. Established with a vision to empower students of North Gujarat, the institute nurtures young talent and develops future IT professionals.

   Our alumni network, spread across Gujarat and beyond, contributes to the institute through active engagement, mentorship, and career support. Over the years, NPCCSM has expanded its reach, ensuring that students not only gain theoretical knowledge but also acquire practical skills that prepare them for higher education, professional success, and tech-driven entrepreneurial ventures.

Courses:

B.C.A.(Bachelor of Computer Application): This programme is aimed at imparting advanced conceptual knowledge and practical skills to the participants to make them acquaint with every areas related to IT and Computer science like hardware, software, programming, languages, networking and applications thereof.


B.B.A.(Bachelor of Business Administration): The BBA aims to provide a career development, programmed for practicing managers to enhance and develop their strategic management and executive skills in an organizational context. The overall aim of the programme is to balance theoretical analysis with practical application, hence developing a critical and questioning approach to the conceptual underpinnings of management.

Placement Cell

The institute has taken initiatives to start the process of placement of the final year students of all group institutes under this trust at a massive scale. Apart from involving the internal faculties from the respective disciplines, experts and trainers from other technical/professional institutes and from the field have been invited to impart the requisite knowledge and skills to the students to prepare them to face the interviews for appropriate jobs in the industries. A placement team consisting of qualified professionals has been set up and is functioning in the process of placement. The team has started interacting with the prospective employers/recruiters, who are expected to visit the campus. Invitations have been extended to leading corporate organizations and industries for conducting campus recruitment which includes pre-placement talks and interviews. The Management has stepped forward to frame out long term strategies to develop and strengthen Institute-Industries Relationship. The campus recruitment/placement process has already commenced. The institute has also taken initiatives to organize a series of in-house training courses/ workshops/seminars/guest lecturers for imparting extra knowledge and providing exposure, practical knowledge and skills to the student community and faculties.
